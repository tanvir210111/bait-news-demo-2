My journey with the Youth Engagement and Support, or YES Club in short, began in my first year. I have been with the club for more than three and a half years. Although it seemed like just a club in the beginning, this organization has become like my family with time.

Not only YES, but all the other clubs in UAP have a different culture. We learn to take responsibility and lead through the club's work. Starting from financial sponsorship to ancillary support, the university authorities are always by our side.

Our YES Club is basically a 'student wing' of Transparency International Bangladesh (TIB). Therefore, many of our activities are conducted in collaboration with TIB, which makes our work more meaningful.

In the last few years, we have done various things including seminars, workshops, campaigns, awareness programs, and anti-corruption plays. For example, we did a play at the club fair last semester, the topic was market syndicates.
